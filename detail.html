description Tóm tắt

In a universe where stars are dying one by one, humanity clings to the fading warmth of the last active sun in the Orion sector. The Galactic Council has forbidden all interstellar travel to conserve energy, grounding the once-great fleets.

Captain Elara Vance, a disgraced pilot with a ship held together by scrap metal and sheer will, stumbles upon a coordinate encrypted in an old pre-collapse transmission. It points to a location that shouldn't exist: a newborn star.

Hunted by the Council's Enforcers and racing against the encroaching dark, Elara must assemble a ragtag crew to find the last spark of hope before the lights go out forever.
